# Orthora Creative Intelligence

Interactive research workspace for Orthora's founder-led creative recovery sprint.

## V1 scope
- 10 competitor brand slots
- 10 creative swipe slots per brand
- Structured teardown: avatar, format, awareness, transcript, angle, hook, lead, mechanism, problem, desire, proof, offer, why it works, Orthora translation
- Manual hypothesis lab
- Angle pipeline: backlog → approved to concept → in creative testing
- Local browser autosave
- JSON export/import backup

## Philosophy
The tool intentionally does **not** automate the research or fill analyses with AI. The founder and creative strategists do the reps. The app provides structure, storage, and progression from research to testable hypotheses.

## Deploy
Zero-build static app. Deploy the repository root directly to Vercel.
